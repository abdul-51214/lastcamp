Privacy policy

Last Camp: Zombie Survival  ·  Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page explains what information the game "Last Camp: Zombie Survival" ("the game") handles, why, and what you can do about it. The game can be played without creating an account and without giving your name.

What the game stores online

  • A guest account. The first time the game is online it creates an account with a random ID. It contains no name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Your Google account details, only if you choose "Sign in with Google". Google then gives us your e-mail address, your Google account ID and the public name and picture of your Google profile. We use them only to recognise your account, so your camp can be restored on another phone. We never see your Google password.
  • Your player name and best scores. The name you type in the game and your best results (nights survived, Blood Moons survived, elite kills, headquarters level) are shown to other players in the rankings.
  • A copy of your saved game. Your camp's progress is copied to your account so it is not lost. Only your own account can read it.
  • Your camp, for raids. So that players can raid each other's camps, other players can see your player name, what your camp looks like (its land, walls, buildings and defences, its headquarters level and how many guards it has) and how many coins and materials it holds. A record of each raid (who attacked whom, when, who won and what was taken) is kept and shown to the two players involved.
  • Play data (you can switch it off). To find out where the game is too hard, too confusing or too slow, the game sends short notes about what happens in it, tied only to your account's random ID: for example "finished the tutorial", "reached chapter mission 6", "survived night 4", "watched a video", how long a session lasted, the phone model, its memory and the frame rate. It never sends your name, e-mail address, location or advertising ID with them. We use them only to improve the game, never for advertising, and never sell or share them. Our reason (legal basis) is our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the game. You can switch it off at any time in SETTINGS > ACCOUNT > PLAY DATA; notes are kept for at most 13 months and are deleted with your account.

Reminders on your phone

If you allow notifications, the game may remind you of things that are really waiting in the game (new daily jobs, a new weekly elite, scavengers who are back): at most one a day and never at night. They are made on your phone; nothing is sent to us to do this. Switch them off in SETTINGS > ACCOUNT > REMINDERS, or in your phone's settings.

Playing with friends

When you play together over the internet, the game connects the phones through a relay service run by Unity Technologies. To do that, Unity processes your device's IP address and a random player ID for the duration of the session. Players in the same camp see the player name you chose.

Voice chat. When you play with friends over the internet, the players in the same camp can talk to each other. Your voice is sent live, through Unity's Vivox voice service, only to the other players in your camp; it is not recorded or stored by us. The game asks your permission before it uses the microphone; if you say no, you can still hear the others. You can mute yourself at any time with the microphone button. To keep the service free, our server counts how many camps are using voice at the same time (the camp's join code and how many players are in it - no voice and no names).

Advertising

The game offers optional video ads: you choose to watch one to get a reward (for example extra coins or getting back up after a defeat). These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the game may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as the phone model, operating system and language) and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first (Google's consent message). You can change your choice later in the game's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • No video plays unless you tap a button to watch it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not read your location, contacts, photos or camera. It uses the microphone only for voice chat with friends, only while you play together, and only if you allow it.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with anyone, apart from the service providers named below, who process it on our behalf.

If in-app purchases are added later, this policy will be updated before they go live.

Service providers

  • Supabase - stores accounts, rankings, saved games and play data.
  • Google - sign-in, only if you choose it, and the optional video ads (AdMob), see "Advertising" above.
  • Unity Technologies - the relay that connects players in multiplayer, and Vivox, which carries the voices in voice chat. The game is built with the Unity engine, which may also collect anonymous device and diagnostic information; see Unity's privacy policy.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and everything stored with it (rankings entry, saved game, camp and raid records, play data) at any time: in the game (SETTINGS > ACCOUNT > DELETE MY ACCOUNT), or by writing to the address below from the e-mail address of your account, or, for a guest account, by telling us your player name. We delete it within 30 days. The steps are also on our account deletion page.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
