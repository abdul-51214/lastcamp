Privacy policy

Last Camp: Zombie Survival  ·  Last updated: 11 October 2026

This page explains what information the game "Last Camp: Zombie Survival" ("the game") handles, why, and what you can do about it. The game can be played without creating an account and without giving your name.

What the game stores online

  • A guest account. The first time the game is online it creates an account with a random ID. It contains no name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Your Google account details, only if you choose "Sign in with Google". Google then gives us your e-mail address, your Google account ID and the public name and picture of your Google profile. We use them only to recognise your account, so your camp can be restored on another phone. We never see your Google password.
  • Your player name and best scores. The name you type in the game and your best results (nights survived, Blood Moons survived, elite kills, headquarters level) are shown to other players in the rankings.
  • A copy of your saved game. Your camp's progress is copied to your account so it is not lost. Only your own account can read it.

Playing with friends

When you play together over the internet, the game connects the phones through a relay service run by Unity Technologies. To do that, Unity processes your device's IP address and a random player ID for the duration of the session. Players in the same camp see the player name you chose.

What the game does not do

  • It does not show advertising and does not use advertising trackers.
  • It does not read your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with anyone, apart from the service providers named below, who process it on our behalf.

If advertising or in-app purchases are added later, this policy will be updated before they go live.

Service providers

  • Supabase - stores accounts, rankings and saved games.
  • Google - sign-in, only if you choose it.
  • Unity Technologies - the relay that connects players in multiplayer. The game is built with the Unity engine, which may also collect anonymous device and diagnostic information; see Unity's privacy policy.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and everything stored with it (rankings entry and saved game) at any time by writing to the address below from the e-mail address of your account, or, for a guest account, by telling us your player name. We delete it within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
