Delete your account

Last Camp: Zombie Survival (by HootCode)  ·  Last updated: 11 November 2026

You can delete your Last Camp account and the information stored with it at any time, in the game or by e-mail. You don't need to give a reason.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open the game and tap the pause button (or go to the title screen).
  2. Tap SETTINGS, then ACCOUNT.
  3. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT, then YES, DELETE IT FOR GOOD.

The account is deleted from our server straight away. The phone needs to be online for this.

Without the game (by e-mail)

Write to lastcamp.game@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and tell us either:

  • the e-mail address of the Google account you signed in with (please write from that address), or
  • for a guest account, the player name you use in the game.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your account (guest or Google sign-in) and your player name,
  • your entry in the rankings,
  • the online copy of your saved game,
  • your camp card (what other players see for raids) and the reports of raids you took part in,
  • the play data the game sent (see the privacy policy),
  • any invitations to play together.

What is not deleted

  • The saved game on your own phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings. (In the game, after deleting the account, your phone plays on as a new guest.)
  • Voice chat is never recorded or stored, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Information Google AdMob collects to show the optional video ads is handled by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
