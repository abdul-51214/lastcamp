Last Camp

Zombie Survival

Scavenge by day. Hold the camp by night. Keep your people alive.

The army left. You didn't. As the last officer in a quarantined city, you start with a campfire and a knife. Sneak through the ruins by day, find guns and survivors, and turn your camp into a fortress. When night falls, the dead come - and every survivor you lose has a name.

Last Camp is a mobile game for Android, in development. You can play alone or defend one camp together with up to three friends.
